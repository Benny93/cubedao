方块岛

CubeDao · fāng kuài dǎo

CubeDao — a block island where everything speaks Mandarin.

Look at things to read their names. Dig, build, farm and cook to hear what you are doing. The 老师 (teacher) in the pavilion and the other villagers ask for things in Chinese; the paths lead to them. The blackboard in the school has exercises. As you learn a word its English disappears, then its pinyin.
